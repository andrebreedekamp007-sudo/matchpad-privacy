Privacy Policy for MatchPad

Last updated: April 2026

MatchPad ("the app") is developed and maintained by Andre Breedekamp ("I", "me", or "my"). This privacy policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Data Collection

MatchPad does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with external servers or third parties. All data created within the app — including player names, match scores, tournament results, and match history — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to me or anyone else.

When using subscription features, purchase transactions are processed by Google Play. If Adapty (our subscription management provider) is enabled, anonymised device identifiers and purchase receipts may be shared with Adapty solely to verify and manage your subscription. No personally identifiable information is shared with Adapty.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

MatchPad does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

3. Bluetooth and Watch Permissions

MatchPad requests Bluetooth permission to:

  • Connect to Bluetooth remote scoring devices (e.g. game controllers used as score clickers)
  • Connect to Samsung Galaxy Watch companions for live score display
  • Connect phones together for multi-court scoring sessions

No data is transmitted externally via Bluetooth. All communication is local between your devices.

4. Audio Permission

MatchPad uses your device's text-to-speech and audio output to announce scores during matches. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted.

5. Photos and Media

MatchPad requests access to your device's images solely to allow you to set an optional profile photo for players in the Player Registry. This image is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded or shared.

6. Internet Access

MatchPad uses an internet connection solely to:

  • Display this Privacy Policy within the app
  • Process and verify subscription purchases via Google Play and Adapty

No personal data, match data, or player data is transmitted over the internet.

7. Storage

All app data is stored locally in the app's private storage on your Android device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Children's Privacy

MatchPad is intended for users aged 18 and over. The app does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

9. Local Network and Bluetooth (Multi-Court Scoring)

When using the multi-court scoring feature, match scores are transmitted directly between devices over a local Bluetooth connection or local Wi-Fi network. This data does not leave your local environment and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

10. Third-Party Services

MatchPad may use the following third-party service:

Adapty — used for subscription management. When active, Adapty may collect anonymised device identifiers and purchase receipt data to verify your subscription status. Adapty does not receive your name, player data, or match history. For more information, see Adapty's Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top.

12. Contact
